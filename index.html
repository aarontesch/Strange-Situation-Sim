Instructor Debrief

Case 1 (Secure B3): High proximity-seeking (6-7), high contact-maintaining (6), minimal avoidance (1), minimal resistance (1), organized behavior (1). Infant balances exploration with secure-base use and recovers readily from mild distress via caregiver contact [1].

Case 2 (Avoidant A1): Low proximity-seeking (2-3), low contact-maintaining (1-2), high avoidance (6-7), minimal resistance (1), organized behavior (1-2). Infant ignores mother despite her reunion attempts—this active disregard is the diagnostic marker [1].

Case 3 (Ambivalent C1): High proximity-seeking (5-6), high contact-maintaining (6-7), minimal avoidance (1), high resistance (5-7), organized behavior (1-2). Infant shows the "push-pull" dynamic: seeks contact urgently but is angry and resistant when held [1].

Case 4 (Disorganized D): Variable scores across proximity, contact, and avoidance; high disorganization (6-7). Infant shows contradictory, apprehensive, or confused behaviors—particularly at reunion—with no coherent strategy [1].
